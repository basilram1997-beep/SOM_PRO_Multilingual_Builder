## Subtitle

Daily school operations for teachers, administrators, and students.

## Description

SOM PRO is a multilingual app for managing daily school operations in one place.

Use it to handle lessons, homework, exams, attendance, behavior, grades, certificates, timetables, and reports.

It is designed to present school information clearly and quickly in a clean interface.
