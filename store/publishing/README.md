# Store Publishing Pack

This folder contains the copy and checklists needed to submit SOM PRO to Google Play and App Store.

## What is ready

- Store listing text in Arabic and English
- Privacy policy draft
- Screenshot checklist
- Release handoff notes
- Final upload pack with the curated screenshot order and final URLs

## What still needs your real values

- App Store / Play Console account details
- Final screenshots from the app

## Recommended order

1. Capture the screenshots listed in `screenshot-checklist.md`.
2. Review `final-upload-pack.md` for the final screenshot order and store URLs.
3. Paste the listing copy into the store consoles.
4. Upload the signed bundle `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`.
5. Complete the store account setup and review steps.
