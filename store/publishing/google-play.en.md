## Title

## Short description

Multilingual school operations platform for teachers, students, and administrators.

## Full description

SOM PRO helps schools manage lessons, homework, exams, attendance, behavior, grades, certificates, timetables, and daily classroom workflows in one place.

The app is designed for school operations and brings daily tasks into a clear multilingual interface.

### Key use cases

- Sign in and manage roles and sessions
- Enter lessons, homework, and exams
- Track attendance and behavior
- Manage grades and certificates
- View timetables, daily program, and reports

### Review notes

- This app is intended for internal school use.
- It does not include advertising or marketing tracking by default.
- School data stays under the institution's privacy and isolation policies.
