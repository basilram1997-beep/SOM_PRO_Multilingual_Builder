# Privacy Policy Draft - SOM PRO

## Scope

This draft is based on the repository privacy requirements and the current school-operations scope.

## Core statement

All school data entered into SOM PRO belongs to the school or educational authority, not to the vendor.

The vendor acts only as a processor/service provider for the contracted educational purpose.

## Data handling principles

- Use school data only for the agreed educational service.
- Do not sell school data.
- Do not reuse school data for marketing.
- Do not share school data with third parties by default.
- Keep school data separate per school.
- Do not store sensitive school data locally in browser storage or on the desktop device except where explicitly allowed for non-sensitive UI preferences or bootstrap data.

## Export and deletion

- Export and deletion must be permission-protected.
- Export and deletion must be logged.
- Export files must not be public.
- Deletion must require explicit confirmation.
- Retention and deletion behavior must follow the contract and the organization policy.

## Third-party services

No third-party service may receive student data unless it is explicitly approved and documented.

## Student media

Photo, video, and audio capture for students is disabled by default and is not part of the current MVP.
