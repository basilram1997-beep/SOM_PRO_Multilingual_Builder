## Core policy points

- School data belongs to the school or authority.
- The vendor processes data only for the agreed educational purpose.
- No advertising use.
- No AI training use without written authorization.
- No default third-party sharing.
- Export, deletion, retention, and breach notification must be governed by written policy.

## Operational note

- If a client or authority needs a different legal clause set, the privacy and terms documents can be adapted without changing the app’s core behavior.
