It is a readiness package, ready for external review and approval, and not a legal certification claim.

## Review goal

The goal is to let a lawyer, privacy reviewer, DPO, security reviewer, or buyer-side assessor verify:

- who controls the data,
- why the data is processed,
- how long it is kept,
- how it is deleted or anonymized,
- what gets exported,
- which third parties are approved,
- whether DPA or BAA handling is needed,
- and what decisions were made during deployment.

## Required approvals

- Privacy policy review.
- Terms review.
- DPA review where applicable.
- BAA review where applicable.
- Retention and deletion policy approval.
- Final commercial or authority sign-off.

## Notes

- If the customer is a HIPAA covered entity or business associate, a BAA must be handled externally.
- If payment card data is ever introduced, the PCI-DSS scope must be reviewed externally before rollout.
- This pack is meant to speed up review, not replace it.
